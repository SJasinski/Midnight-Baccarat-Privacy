This policy explains how Midnight Baccarat handles information in its Android app and how information is handled when you contact us or visit this policy page.

About the app

Midnight Baccarat is an offline simulated casino game for entertainment and learning, intended for adults aged 18 and over. Practice chips have no real-world monetary value and cannot be purchased, redeemed, transferred, or exchanged for money or prizes. Practice results do not predict real gambling outcomes.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your practice-chip balance, selected wager and bet, tutorial preference, recent completed hands (up to 50), last completed round, and remaining card-shoe order in app-private storage on your device. This information lets you resume your session and view hand history.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, automatic crash reporting, billing, or an Internet permission. It does not send your session data to SJasinski or third parties. Android and your device or app-store provider may separately process information under their own settings and policies.

Retention, reset, and deletion

Local session information remains until you replace or delete it. New session clears hand history, refills practice chips, and shuffles a new shoe; it retains your tutorial preference. To remove all saved app data and preferences, use Android Settings to clear the app's storage. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data. The app disables Android cloud backup and excludes its saved preferences from Android 12 and later cloud backup and device transfer.

When you contact us

If you email midnightbaccarat@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any name you provide, and your message and attachments. We use this information to answer your request, investigate issues, and handle privacy inquiries. Your game session is not automatically attached. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, or unrelated personal information.

Support correspondence is handled through Gmail, which is operated by Google under its privacy policy. We retain correspondence only as needed to resolve and follow up on requests or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising; information may be disclosed when required by law.

External links and this website

Privacy and support links open your browser or email application. Those services handle information under their own policies. Opening a link does not upload your saved game session.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Pages documentation and privacy statement. This page does not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact us so we can address it and delete the information as appropriate.

Security and your choices

The app uses Android app-private storage for local session information. No storage or communication method is completely secure. You control local data through the reset and deletion options above. For questions or requests about information you have sent us, contact midnightbaccarat@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Any future features that change data handling will require a corresponding policy update.
